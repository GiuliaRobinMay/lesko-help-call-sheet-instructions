## Where the timings come from

Not invented — checked against outside sources:

- Confirm receipt **3–5 business days** after applying, then let their window run;
  repeated calling during the window is counterproductive
- **30–60 days** is a normal decision window for this kind of assistance
- Requests for more information carry **very short deadlines** (SSA allows about 10
  days), and a late or partial reply is usually an automatic denial
- Send everything requested in **one** response; keep a copy and proof of delivery
- Most denials trace to missing or incomplete documents
